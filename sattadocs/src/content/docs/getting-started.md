# Getting Started

Welcome to SattaSpace! This guide will help you understand how to get started with our platform and make the most of its features.

## Quick Start

If you're a **user** looking to access SattaSpace services:

1. **Create an Account** — Visit [SattaBase](https://base.sattaspace.com) and register with your email
2. **Verify Your Email** — Check your inbox for a verification code
3. **Complete Your Profile** — Add your name, timezone, and preferences
4. **Choose a Plan** — Browse available plans and subscribe

If you're a **developer** integrating with SattaSpace:

1. **Explore the Documentation** — Understand the available APIs and webhooks
2. **Set Up Your Environment** — Configure your application to work with SattaSpace
3. **Test Integration** — Use our sandbox environment for development
4. **Go Live** — Deploy your integration to production

- **Billing** — Subscription management and transaction history

## Key Concepts

### Email Verification

Email verification is required before subscribing to paid plans. After registration, you'll receive a 6-digit code valid for 10 minutes. You can resend the code after a 60-second cooldown.

### Remember Me

During sign-in, the "Remember me" option controls session persistence:

- **Unchecked**: Session is per-tab; closing the tab signs you out
- **Checked**: Session persists for 7 days across browser restarts

### Identity Confirmation

Sensitive operations (like account deletion) require re-entering your password. This confirmation is valid for 5 minutes, adding an extra security layer.

### Proration

When changing plans mid-cycle, charges or credits are prorated:

- **Upgrades**: You're charged the prorated difference immediately
- **Downgrades**: Credits are applied to your next invoice

## Security Best Practices

1. **Use a Strong Password** — At least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
2. **Enable Remember Me Only on Trusted Devices** — Avoid using it on public computers
3. **Verify Your Email Promptly** — Verification codes expire after 10 minutes
4. **Review Active Sessions** — Check Settings for any suspicious activity
5. **Export Your Data Regularly** — Keep backups of your billing information
